Fullscreen & 16:9 Aspect Lock

For accurate AR FOV spatial simulation on a 2D widescreen display, please press F11 (or Fn + F11) on your keyboard to enter browser Fullscreen Widescreen Mode, or click below.

Hardware Aspect Ratio Disclaimer: Target AR smart glasses (such as XREAL Aura and VITURE Luma Ultra) feature 16:10 aspect ratio Micro-OLED displays (1920×1200 per eye), while VR/MR headsets (such as Samsung XR, Quest 3, and Quest 3S) feature near-square optics (~15:16). Locking your browser to 16:9 widescreen mode provides the standard baseline viewport to simulate all target hardware optics, FOV bounding frames, and spatial UI coordinates without geometric stretching or pillarboxing.

FOSS Open-Source Developer Notice

MIT Licensed • Open Source Concept Repository

AuraBeat is a spatial rhythm engine concept designed for AR smart glasses and spatial XR headsets. In this proof-of-concept prototype, the spatial UI/UX framework, FOV calibration models, and interaction paradigms are simulated on 2D widescreen displays. This design structure serves as the foundation to be deployed and optimized on physical spatial devices—such as the XREAL Aura, VITURE Luma Ultra, Samsung XR, and Meta Quest headsets—as developer hardware becomes available to test on physical optics.


Currently simulated on 2D screens, the underlying spatial audio mechanics, 6DoF hand tracking, and spatial mesh anchoring are mapped via telemetry profiles. Active development is ongoing, with full on-device hardware integration, low-latency spatial rendering, and physical sensor tuning scheduled to commence upon receiving target physical hardware during upcoming developer access cohorts.
