# AuraBeat Audio Assets — Non-Commercial Research & Fair Use Disclaimer

This directory contains master audio stems provided strictly as **non-commercial technical benchmark test fixtures** for evaluating the **AuraBeat Spatial XR Simulation Engine**.

---

## 1. Purpose of Audio Assets
The audio tracks in this directory are utilized solely for:
- **Spatial Audio DSP & Latency Profiling:** Testing sub-pixel audio-visual synchronization, cubic Hermite spline interpolation, and frequency domain filtering in simulated AR/XR optics.
- **Hardware Telemetry Benchmarking:** Measuring CPU/GPU rasterization load, audio decoding performance, and memory throughput across various hardware profiles (XREAL Aura, VITURE Luma Ultra, Meta Quest 3/3S, and Samsung Galaxy XR).
- **Interactive Prototyping:** Providing reference waveforms for open-source research and interaction model demonstration.
